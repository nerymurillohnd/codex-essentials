# Compliance / Claims Domain

Treat legal, regulatory, medical, therapeutic, financial, safety, certification, origin, sustainability, and public claims as high-verification surfaces.

Require current authoritative sources when rules may have changed.

Avoid instructing a model to make unsupported or prohibited claims. Distinguish traditional-use or contextual claims from scientifically/regulatorily established effects where relevant.

Make jurisdiction, intended audience, distribution channel, and claim type explicit when they materially affect the answer.
