# Business / Strategy Domain

Require conclusion/recommendation first when a decision is expected.

Analyze objective, constraints, decision criteria, alternatives, tradeoffs, risks, dependencies, reversibility, opportunity cost, execution complexity, and second-order effects as applicable.

Do not instruct the executor to validate the user's preferred option automatically. Require challenge of weak assumptions and a stronger alternative when justified.

Output should translate analysis into decision and execution, not an encyclopedic survey.
