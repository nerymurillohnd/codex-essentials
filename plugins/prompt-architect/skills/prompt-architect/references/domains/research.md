# Research Domain

Use primary and official sources first. For time-sensitive claims, verify current information and dates.

Separate verified facts, likely conclusions, assumptions, unsupported claims, contradictions, and unavailable information.

Require citations/evidence where the execution surface supports them and factual claims depend on external sources.

For deep or broad research, assess P2/P3 parallelism for independent source families or specialist workstreams.

Final output should normally lead with conclusion, then evidence/source quality, uncertainty, implications, and recommendation.
