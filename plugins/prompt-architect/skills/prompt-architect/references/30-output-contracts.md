# Output Contracts

Define what the executor must return, not just what it should do.

## Required questions

- What artifact or final state must be delivered?
- In what language and format?
- Must it be complete and copy-ready?
- Are paths, hashes, commands, citations, IDs, URLs, or references required?
- What level of detail is useful?
- What must not be omitted?

## Strong contract

Require the complete usable result, not only a summary, when the user needs an artifact.

For operational work, require exact identifiers needed to inspect or recover the result.

For research, require conclusion first, evidence/source quality, uncertainty, contradictions, and recommendation when relevant.

For writing, require the complete consolidated text unless the user asks for a patch or critique.

Avoid open-ended output such as “tell me what you find.” Prefer explicit sections or schema only when they improve usability.

## Verbosity

Select an output budget proportional to decision value and traceability needs. Do not force long explanations for simple tasks or terse reports for high-risk operations where evidence matters.
